# Vedant — TurboQuant completed Semester 1 work

Implemented TurboQuant-MSE and product/QJL key quantization, bit packing and metadata accounting, protected BF16 prefill, and integration into the team's shared kvpress LongBench harness. Completed nine fixed settings on the frozen 800-record development set (7,200 predictions). Completed real-query calibration on 16 examples covering all tasks (512 per-layer reports), followed by one full adaptive experiment (800 predictions). All 8,000 predictions have been independently re-scored with no mismatch, OOM or blank response.

The team's full-cache baseline is 47.72. Fixed MSE-key 3-bit and 3.5-bit means are 47.90 and 47.84; these small mean differences do not establish superiority. The adaptive allocation chose 3-bit QJL in all 32 layers, with zero calibration-objective reduction. On the shared 768 held-out records it scored 44.4970 versus the earlier uniform 3-bit run's 44.4096; this does not demonstrate adaptive improvement.

Computed packed-cache size is reported separately from measured GPU peak allocation. Decoded BF16 cache remains resident; no physical VRAM savings or serving speedup is established. Mixed-channel rate allocation and protected generated tokens are explicit team-protocol adaptations. These are development-set results, not an untouched final test benchmark or a full reproduction of every paper experiment.
